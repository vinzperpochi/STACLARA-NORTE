Barangay Records Privacy Notice

This system processes personal information for legitimate barangay administration, public service delivery, document issuance, health and social-welfare assistance, disaster response, and statutory recordkeeping. Processing is designed to follow the principles of transparency, legitimate purpose, proportionality, and accountability under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and its implementing rules.

Information we collect

We collect only information reasonably needed for the selected service, such as identity and contact details, household or service information, request history, and records required for health, welfare, emergency, or legal operations.

How information is protected

Access is role-based and authenticated. Passwords are hashed, forms use CSRF protection, sessions expire, uploads are restricted and renamed, database queries are parameterized, sensitive pages are not cached, and sensitive actions are recorded in an append-only audit trail.

Public verification

Document verification exposes only the minimum facts needed to confirm authenticity. It does not disclose the resident's name, address, contact information, health information, or other sensitive personal information.

Your rights

Subject to applicable legal limitations, data subjects may request information about processing, access or correction of their information, and raise concerns about unauthorized or excessive processing through the barangay office or its designated privacy contact.

Retention and sharing

Records are retained only as long as necessary for the stated purpose, legal obligations, and operational accountability. Information is shared only with authorized personnel or parties with a lawful and documented purpose.
